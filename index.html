About Us

Lenovo Group Limited is a global technology powerhouse and the world's largest personal computer vendor, operating in more than 180 markets worldwide. Founded in Beijing in 1984 as Legend and later rebranded, the company achieved global prominence in 2005 by acquiring IBM’s personal computer division, which brought the iconic ThinkPad brand into its portfolio. Today, Lenovo has dual headquarters in Beijing, China, and Morrisville, North Carolina, and has expanded far beyond PCs into smartphones through its acquisition of Motorola Mobility, as well as enterprise servers, cloud storage, and AI-driven tech solutions. Known for balancing business-grade reliability with consumer innovation, the company continues to lead the tech industry across its diverse product ecosystem, spanning ThinkPad, IdeaPad, Yoga, and Legion devices.
